Module 1 — The Myth of the Final War

Theme: Deconstructing AI vs Humanity

Topics

Why dominance narratives persist

Intelligence vs power

Optimization vs wisdom

Key Insight

Fear of AI is fear of losing moral responsibility.

Assignment

Write a short reflection:

What story about the future did you inherit?

Who benefits from that story?

Module 2 — Human Limits Are Not Failures

Theme: Biology, cognition, and planetary scale

Topics

Cognitive overload

Political time horizons

Emotional saturation

Why humans cannot manage global systems alone

Exercise

Map one global system (energy, food, waste)

Module 3 — Waste Is an Ethical Problem

Theme: From engineering to morality

Topics

Externalities as delayed harm

Pollution as invisible violence

Circular vs extractive design

Case Study

Trace a single object (plastic bottle, smartphone)
→ origin
→ use
→ waste
→ planetary impact

Module 4 — AI as Caretaker, Not Ruler

Theme: Redefining optimization

Topics

Stewardship-based optimization

Constraint-first AI design

Why humanoid robotics matter

Human veto authority

Long-horizon goals

Module 5 — The Surrender of Destructive Labor

Theme: Liberation, not replacement

Topics

Dangerous and dehumanizing work

Module 6 — Planetary Rights

Theme: Earth as a protected system

Topics

Dependency-based ethics

Module 7 — Why Art Cannot Be Optimized

Theme: Preserving sacred inefficiency

Topics

Art vs content

Mistakes as culture

Meaning beyond metrics

Moral agency

Consent

Accountability
